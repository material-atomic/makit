Config playground

Start from a preset, add blocks, and watch shield.yaml take shape. Every change is checked by makit's own config check, compiled to WebAssembly with the bundled catalog — the same messages as makit shield config check on your server. Nothing leaves your browser; secrets stay placeholders unless you type them.
